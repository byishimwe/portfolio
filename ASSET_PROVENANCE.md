The layout around those components is a local capture harness, not a claimed production account session. The composer is disabled, the demonstration identity is “Campus Demo,” and the preview’s local API only returns zero/empty demonstration state. It does not claim those are real users or live activity. The source projects were not edited. The relevant disclosure appears in the public Quad case study and image captions.

No AI-generated screen approximations or stock artwork were required. Fresh authentic captures and CSS editorial framing were the appropriate treatment for this portfolio.
